Babytooth is an art collective and curatorial project. A location-independent platform hosting exhibitions and events, it creates a flexible, in-between space and cultural lab exploring the balance between sincerity and irony. Babytooth aims to provide a third space where experimentation, community, and creative exchange coexist.

We treat collective belief as a tangible material. An egregore is a thoughtform born from shared attention, holding communities together much like a myth. Our framework draws from the loose folklore of the milk tooth, a near-universal custom whose mascot shifts constantly across depictions without breaking the spell.

Everything we build tests how tending to these small, unfixed fictions cultivates IRL belonging. From rotating online exhibitions and interactive media to collectible art objects, we design spaces to be used and inhabited, giving people room to participate, gather, and keep the myth alive.

The {Dollhouse} is an online environment and part of The {Chatroom} & The {Dollhouse}, a hybrid art project developed for The Wrong Biennale 2025–26.

The {Dollhouse} exists at thedoll.house as a poetic webspace where collectible objects open portals. Each participant is assigned a /room/ where their artwork lives.

The website was developed between November 1st, 2025 and March 31st, 2026.
