Babytooth is an independent curatorial and artistic platform founded by Sarah Khadra Hasni, working across exhibitions, games, online environments, and participatory formats to explore how collective belief shapes objects, narratives, and communities.

Our flagship project, The Chatroom & The Dollhouse (The Wrong Biennale, 2025–26), brought together 29 artists across an online exhibition, a self-designed tabletop game and a physical group show — treating each format as a different entry point into the same shared world.

Babytooth's ongoing work sits deliberately between disciplines: not quite gallery, not quite game studio, not quite publisher, built instead around the conviction that participation and folklore are two names for the same instinct.
